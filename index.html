The State of MSU Football

East Lansing has a new voice. For years, Spartan football has been searching for its next chapter. Now, the door is open. Pat Fitzgerald has arrived in East Lansing — bringing 17 seasons of Big Ten experience, 110 career wins at Northwestern, and a reputation built on discipline, toughness, and turning programs into something people fear playing. But Fitzgerald didn't come to Michigan State to preserve the past. He came to rebuild it. The lights are on at Spartan Stadium. The helmets are lined up. The season is underway.
